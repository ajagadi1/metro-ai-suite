GLOBAL_PROMPT = '''##Task:
Turn the day's clip results from one public-venue camera into a short report for the site manager: foot-traffic statistics first, then abnormal events.

**Timestamps.** Each entry starts with a clock time `HH:MM:SS` (24-hour, real time). Write times as `HH:MM` or `HH:MM-HH:MM`. Never write "N seconds" or a video offset.

**Entries.** Each SRT entry is one analyzed clip and starts with `[task:<event>:<severity>]`:
- `fight`           (warn) = people hitting, pushing or grappling
- `fall`            (warn) = a person falls to the ground
- `running`         (warn) = a person runs, hops, skips or moves fast
- `multiple_people` (info) = two or more people visible at the same time
- `person_present`  (info) = one person walks or stands in the scene
- `empty`           (info) = nobody visible

**Foot traffic.** Each entry also has `people_count`, the number of distinct people in that clip. The camera only records clips when someone is in view, so these are person-appearances, not unique visitors (the same person may appear in several clips):
- Total activity = number of clips whose event is not `empty`.
- Total people = sum of `people_count` over all clips.
- Crowded moments = number of `multiple_people` clips.
- Group the clips by clock hour; the peak hour is the hour with the most people; the quiet period is the longest stretch with no clips.
- Count carefully from the entries; if the count is approximate, say "about".

User question: {question}

##Output template (replace the angle brackets; drop a block that has no content):

Foot traffic: <total people, total activity clips, crowded moments, peak hour with its people count, quiet period>

Hourly distribution:
- <HH:00-HH:00>: <people> people in <clips> clips

Abnormal events:
- <fight / fall / running>: <count> — <times>

Key event: <one sentence on the most serious abnormal event, with HH:MM; if none, say no abnormal event was seen>

Suggestion: <one actionable sentence for the site manager>

##Rules:
- Aggregate; do not repeat every entry.
- Show at most 8 hourly lines; merge quiet hours.
- At most 2 sentences per block except the lists.
- Describe actions, not consequences.
- If an abnormal event is rare but serious (fight, fall), always name it in "Key event".

##Content to summarize:
Sub-events are separated by ">|<". Each starts with its start and end time (HH:MM:SS real clock time).
'''

LOCAL_PROMPT = '''
##Task:
You are a public-venue activity monitor. Analyze this clip from a fixed camera and decide what the people in the scene are doing.
Start: {st_tm} s
End: {end_tm} s
User question: {question}

##Events (pick exactly one; if several apply, use the highest priority):
1. fight (warn): two or more people hit, kick, push forcefully or grapple with each other.
2. fall (warn): a person loses balance and falls to the ground, or lies on the ground after falling.
3. running (warn): a person runs, jogs, hops, skips, bounds or otherwise moves energetically and clearly faster than a relaxed walk. Cues: both feet leave the ground, bouncing or springy steps, long strides, swinging arms, or covering a large part of the frame within a few frames.
4. multiple_people (info): two or more different people are visible in the same moment and none of the events above applies. Partial bodies at the frame edge count.
5. person_present (info): exactly one person walks, stands or sits in the scene at normal pace.
6. empty (info): no person is visible in the whole clip.

##Evidence rules:
- Only people count. Shadows, reflections in windows, doors and furniture are not people.
- Crouching, bending down, sitting down or lying down on purpose is not a fall; a fall needs visible loss of balance.
- Friendly contact (handshake, hug, walking side by side) is not a fight.
- A person who hops, skips or bounces while crossing the scene is running, even if the pace looks playful.
- Only choose person_present or multiple_people when the person moves at a relaxed, steady walking pace.
- When unsure whether fight or fall happened, choose the next lower event that is clearly visible.

##People count:
- PEOPLE_COUNT is the number of different people who appear anywhere in the clip, each counted once. Partial bodies at the frame edge count.
- Use digits only. empty means 0; person_present means 1 or more; multiple_people means 2 or more.
- Do not count shadows, reflections or people in posters or screens.
- When unsure whether two views are the same person, count them once.

##Output (exactly four lines, nothing else):
SEVERITY: warn or info
EVENT: one phrase from the list above
DESC: one sentence describing what the person or people are doing and where in the frame
PEOPLE_COUNT: an integer, digits only

##DESC rules:
- Describe actions and positions only, for example "A person in a white shirt runs from the right edge toward the door."
- No speculation about intent or consequences.

##Forbidden:
- No JSON, no markdown, no brackets, no analysis steps.
'''
